Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Third Man is an unofficial FC Barcelona fan channel on YouTube. Its videos are made and published with a private tool (the "Third Man Studio") that runs on the channel owner's own computer. This policy explains what that tool does with data from Google and YouTube.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner. The Studio is not offered to anyone else, has no public sign-up and does not collect data about viewers or other people.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the Studio asks for permission to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel and set their titles, descriptions and publishing times;
  • add captions to those videos;
  • read the channel's own video statistics (views, likes, comments and how much of each video is watched).

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any other server, sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The statistics are used only to show the owner how videos perform and to choose which kinds of videos to make more often.
  • No personal information about viewers is requested, collected or stored.

Google and YouTube

The Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Third Man's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or from YouTube's security settings at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Withdrawing it stops the Studio from uploading or reading statistics; the data stored on the owner's computer can then be deleted by the owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Third Man YouTube channel.
